Vetting Walsh Classroom.

Everything a district privacy or technology office usually asks for, in one place: what's collected, where it goes, what to allow in your filter, and how to get a signed data privacy agreement.

At a glance

What it is
Free educational web apps for media arts classes (screenwriting, photography, animation, video). No paid tier.
Operator
Tom Walsh, an individual teacher. Contact help@walshclassroom.com.
Advertising, analytics, tracking
None. No ad networks, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party cookies.
Sale or sharing of data
Never sold, rented, traded or shared for anyone else's purposes.
AI
No AI features process student data, and student data is never used to train AI.
Sign-in
Email and password (Google Firebase Authentication). Apps that save nothing, like Guide Match, need no account.
Encryption
HTTPS everywhere. Passwords stored hashed by Google.
Data agreement
I'll sign the Massachusetts Student Data Privacy Agreement or the National Data Privacy Agreement (Student Data Privacy Consortium), including the Exhibit E general offer to other districts.

Getting a signed agreement

  1. Email help@walshclassroom.com with your district's name and the agreement you use. Massachusetts districts usually use the Massachusetts Student Data Privacy Agreement from the Student Data Privacy Consortium (SDPC); others use the National Data Privacy Agreement.
  2. I'll complete and sign it, including the data schedule above, and the Exhibit E general offer so other districts can adopt the same terms.
  3. If your district already has an SDPC agreement on file for Walsh Classroom, you can sign its Exhibit E to adopt it without a new negotiation.

Requests about student data

Schools can ask for a student's or a class's data to be exported or deleted at any time. Deletion is completed within 30 days of a request, and at the end of an agreement as it specifies, with written confirmation. Email help@walshclassroom.com from a school address.

Security incidents

If student data is accessed without authorization, affected schools are told promptly with what happened, what data was involved and what's being done, following the notice terms in the school's agreement and applicable law, including Massachusetts General Laws chapter 93H.

Accessibility

The hub is built for keyboard use and screen readers, with high-contrast colors in light and dark themes and text set in Atkinson Hyperlegible. Report anything that gets in the way to help@walshclassroom.com.
