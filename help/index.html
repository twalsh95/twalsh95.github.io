The basics

Which apps need an account?

Apps that save your work need you to sign in. Each app's card on the home page says "Sign in required" if it does. Games like Guide Match don't save anything, so they don't need an account.

Which browser should I use?

Any up-to-date browser works: Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. School Chromebooks are fine.

Can I work from home?

Yes. Go to walshclassroom.com on any computer, open the app, and sign in. Your work is saved to your account, not to the computer.

The page looks broken or won't load.

Refresh the page first. If that doesn't fix it, close the tab and open the app again from walshclassroom.com. Still broken? Tell your teacher which app, and what you were doing when it broke.

What's the bar at the top of each app?

That's the Walsh Classroom bar. Use it to get back to the list of apps or to this help page from inside any app.
